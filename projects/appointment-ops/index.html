Sample Project

Appointment Ops Automation

Booking reminders, prep notes, call templates, and post-call next steps for smoother appointments.

Demo walkthrough available on request. Contact me
Problem

Calls create scattered prep work and missed follow-ups.

Manual appointment operations make it easy to forget reminders, call notes, or next-step messages after meetings.

Solution

A calendar-based workflow that prepares and follows up automatically.

Every booking can trigger reminders, a notes template, CRM context, and a post-call next-step task or email.

Technical Approach

Calendar trigger, reminder timing, notes template, and CRM update.

The system connects Calendly or Google Calendar with Gmail, Notion, CRM fields, and automation logic in n8n.

Outcomes

More reliable meetings and cleaner next steps.

Appointments become easier to prepare for, easier to document, and easier to follow up after.
