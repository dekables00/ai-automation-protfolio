Sample Project

AI Reporting Automation

Weekly data collection, ChatGPT or Gemini summarization, dashboard updates, and team digest delivery in one repeatable system.

Demo walkthrough available on request. Contact me
Problem

Reports take too long when numbers and notes are collected manually.

Teams spend valuable time pulling data from multiple tools, writing summaries, and sending status updates.

Solution

An automated reporting loop that turns raw data into a clear weekly digest.

Data is pulled into a sheet, summarized with ChatGPT or Gemini, added to a dashboard, and shared with the team through email or chat.

Technical Approach

Scheduled data pull, prompt template, dashboard sync, and digest delivery.

The workflow uses scheduled triggers, Google Sheets, AI summary prompts for ChatGPT or Gemini, and notification steps with error checks. Codex can help with API logic and webhook troubleshooting when the setup needs custom code.

Outcomes

Faster reporting with less copy-paste work.

Your team receives consistent weekly insight without rebuilding the same report from scratch.
